Tournament Rules

Tees
Women→Red Seniors→Gold All others→White
3-Player Teams Short a player? One teammate doubles up each hole — hitting twice per shot to keep pace with the field. The 4th mulligan goes to whoever is doubling up. Rotate every hole.

Prizes

Team Lowest Score — Personalized Titleist Pro V1s for each player on the winning team.
Individual Closest to Pin — Holes 4 & 16 — Bear Slide pro shop credit
Individual Straightest Drive — Hole 8 — Bear Slide pro shop credit

Scorecards

Use Bear Slide scorecards. Record your team's score on each hole and turn in at the end of the round.

What's in Your Pack

  • 4 Mulligan Cards
  • 1 Cheat Code Hole Card
  • 1 Lifeline (string)

Mulligan

4 cards · one per player

Each player on your foursome receives one mulligan card. Use it anytime during the round to replay any shot — an errant drive, a chunked approach, or a putt that burned the edge.

Tear your card when you use it. One use per player, no exceptions.

Cheat Code Hole

1 card · one use

Choose one hole (any par 4 or par 5 on the course) where your entire team tees off from a preferred forward position instead of the standard tee box:

  • Par 4 — team tees off from 150 yards out from the center of the green.
  • Par 5 — team tees off from 200 yards out from the center of the green.

Declare before the hole begins. Tear the card when used. Use once during the round.

The Lifeline

1 string · one use

Your randomly assigned length of string (6–24 inches). At any point during the round, if your ball finishes within the string's length of the cup, it counts as holed — no additional shot needed.

Measure from the edge of the cup. Use once during the round.

50/50 Raffle Tickets available throughout the round on course. Drawing held at the end of the round.

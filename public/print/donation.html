Sam Lower Memorial Golf Tournament · May 15, 2026

Played in Memory of Sam Lower

Help Us Beat Multiple Myeloma.

Today's tournament is raising money for research that could change — and save — lives. You don't have to be playing to make a difference.

Sam Lower was a beloved father, husband, and friend who passed away from Multiple Myeloma — a blood cancer that affects plasma cells in bone marrow. He loved golf, he loved people, and he made everyone around him better. This tournament is our way of honoring his memory and fighting back against the disease that took him from us.
